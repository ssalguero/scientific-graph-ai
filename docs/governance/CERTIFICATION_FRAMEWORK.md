# Certification Framework

## Overview

Certification Framework defines the official process through which Scientific Graph AI validates architectural maturity, engineering quality and Release readiness.

Certification provides objective confirmation that project standards have been satisfied.

Every official Release requires successful certification.

---

## Certification Objectives

Certification exists to:

- validate architectural integrity;
- confirm governance compliance;
- verify implementation quality;
- ensure Release readiness;
- preserve long-term maintainability.

---

## Certification Philosophy

Certification is evidence-based.

Architectural quality shall be demonstrated rather than assumed.

Certification represents the formal conclusion of every significant project milestone.

---

## Certification Levels

### Architecture Certification

Verifies:

- architectural consistency;
- domain ownership;
- dependency compliance;
- governance alignment.

---

### Domain Certification

Verifies:

- domain responsibilities;
- public contracts;
- implementation boundaries;
- ownership preservation.

---

### Integration Certification

Verifies:

- interoperability;
- workflow execution;
- cross-domain communication;
- interface stability.

---

### Performance Certification

Verifies:

- performance objectives;
- scalability;
- optimization;
- benchmark compliance.

---

### Documentation Certification

Verifies:

- roadmap consistency;
- governance documentation;
- terminology;
- architectural documentation.

---

### Release Certification

Verifies:

- completion of every Quality Gate;
- successful certification activities;
- Production readiness.

---

---

## Certification Principles

Certification follows these permanent principles.

- Objective evaluation.
- Evidence-based verification.
- Architecture before implementation.
- Governance compliance.
- Complete documentation.
- Traceable results.

---

## Certification Evidence

Certification may include:

- validation reports;
- architectural reviews;
- governance approvals;
- Quality Gate reports;
- benchmark results;
- documentation reviews.

Every certification shall remain traceable.

---

## Success Criteria

Certification is considered successful when:

- architectural integrity is verified;
- governance compliance is confirmed;
- documentation is complete;
- Release readiness is demonstrated;
- Production approval is granted.

---

## Long-Term Evolution

Future certification capabilities may include:

- automated certification pipelines;
- governance dashboards;
- AI-assisted compliance analysis;
- continuous certification monitoring.

The Certification Framework shall remain the authoritative quality assurance model governing Scientific Graph AI.

---

## Conclusion

Certification Framework establishes the permanent certification process supporting the long-term evolution of Scientific Graph AI.

Every future Release shall demonstrate compliance with this framework before official publication.
