# Decision Framework

## Overview

Decision Framework defines the official process through which architectural, technical and strategic decisions are evaluated within Scientific Graph AI.

Its purpose is to ensure that every significant decision follows a transparent, repeatable and evidence-based process.

---

## Decision Philosophy

Architectural decisions shall be based upon documented principles rather than personal preference.

Every approved decision shall strengthen the platform.

Consistency takes precedence over convenience.

---

## Decision Categories

Scientific Graph AI recognizes four categories of decisions.

### Architectural Decisions

Examples include:

- new domains;
- dependency changes;
- public contracts;
- platform restructuring.

---

### Technical Decisions

Examples include:

- implementation strategies;
- infrastructure improvements;
- performance optimization;
- engineering standards.

---

### Product Decisions

Examples include:

- workflow evolution;
- UX improvements;
- scientific capabilities;
- platform expansion.

---

### Governance Decisions

Examples include:

- policy updates;
- certification rules;
- quality procedures;
- documentation standards.

---

## Official Decision Process

Every significant decision follows the same lifecycle.

Problem Identification

↓

Context Analysis

↓

Architectural Evaluation

↓

Alternative Assessment

↓

Impact Analysis

↓

Governance Review

↓

Approval

↓

Implementation

↓

Documentation Update

↓

Certification

---

## Evaluation Criteria

Every proposal shall be evaluated according to:

- architectural consistency;
- domain ownership;
- dependency integrity;
- scientific correctness;
- maintainability;
- scalability;
- backward compatibility;
- governance compliance.

---

## Decision Principles

Every approved decision shall satisfy the following principles.

### Consistency

The decision aligns with established architecture.

---

### Simplicity

The simplest acceptable solution is preferred.

---

### Sustainability

The decision supports long-term evolution.

---

### Transparency

Decision rationale shall remain documented.

---

### Reversibility

Whenever practical, architectural decisions should remain reversible.

---

## Escalation Rules

Formal governance review is mandatory for:

- new domains;
- ownership modifications;
- dependency exceptions;
- breaking public contracts;
- architectural restructuring;
- governance policy changes.

Routine implementation decisions remain within normal engineering activities.

---

## Documentation Requirements

Every significant decision shall record:

- problem statement;
- evaluated alternatives;
- selected solution;
- architectural justification;
- expected impact;
- governance approval.

Decision history becomes part of the permanent architectural knowledge of Scientific Graph AI.

---

## Success Criteria

Decision Framework is considered successful when:

- decisions remain consistent;
- governance becomes predictable;
- rationale is preserved;
- architectural quality improves;
- technical debt decreases.

---

## Conclusion

Decision Framework provides the official methodology governing architectural decision-making within Scientific Graph AI.

Future platform evolution shall continue following this documented process.
