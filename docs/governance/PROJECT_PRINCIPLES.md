# Project Principles

## Overview

Project Principles establish the permanent engineering, architectural and governance philosophy of Scientific Graph AI.

These principles define the foundation upon which every architectural decision, implementation activity and long-term strategic evolution shall be based.

They remain valid independently of technologies, frameworks or implementation details.

---

## Vision

Scientific Graph AI is an architecture-driven scientific platform designed for long-term evolution.

The project prioritizes:

- Scientific Integrity
- Architectural Consistency
- Engineering Excellence
- Sustainable Innovation
- Continuous Improvement

Every future contribution shall reinforce these objectives.

---

## Core Principles

### Architecture First

Architecture defines implementation.

Implementation shall never redefine architecture.

---

### Scientific Integrity

Scientific correctness takes precedence over implementation convenience.

---

### Single Source of Truth

Every architectural responsibility has one—and only one—authoritative owner.

---

### Separation of Concerns

Responsibilities remain isolated.

Domains shall never assume responsibilities belonging to other domains.

---

### Explicit Ownership

Every architectural responsibility belongs to one domain.

Ownership shall remain explicit throughout the platform.

---

### Explicit Dependencies

Dependencies shall remain documented, intentional and governed.

Implicit coupling is prohibited.

---

### Documentation as Architecture

Documentation represents the official architectural intent of Scientific Graph AI.

Implementation shall remain aligned with certified documentation.

---

### Governance Before Change

Architectural evolution requires documented governance.

Uncontrolled architectural drift is prohibited.

---

### Continuous Evolution

Scientific Graph AI evolves incrementally while preserving architectural stability.

---

### Long-Term Maintainability

Engineering decisions prioritize sustainability over short-term optimization.

---

## Engineering Philosophy

Scientific Graph AI favors:

- clarity over complexity;
- consistency over convenience;
- architecture over shortcuts;
- quality over speed;
- sustainability over temporary optimization.

These values guide every engineering decision.

---

## Success Criteria

Project Principles are considered successful when:

- architectural consistency is preserved;
- engineering decisions remain predictable;
- documentation reflects implementation;
- long-term maintainability improves;
- scientific integrity remains protected.

---

## Conclusion

Project Principles define the permanent engineering philosophy governing Scientific Graph AI.

Every future architectural, governance and implementation decision shall remain aligned with these principles.
