# Architecture Governance

## Overview

Architecture Governance defines the permanent framework responsible for preserving the architectural integrity of Scientific Graph AI.

Its objective is to ensure that architectural evolution remains intentional, documented and aligned with the long-term vision established by MASTER_ROADMAP_V2.

Architecture Governance protects the platform from uncontrolled architectural evolution.

---

## Governance Objectives

Architecture Governance exists to:

- preserve architectural consistency;
- protect domain ownership;
- control architectural evolution;
- reduce long-term technical debt;
- support sustainable platform growth;
- maintain architectural documentation.

---

## Governance Authority

Architecture Governance is responsible for:

- reviewing architectural proposals;
- approving structural modifications;
- validating dependency changes;
- protecting public contracts;
- maintaining architectural documentation;
- supervising architectural certification.

Governance authority remains independent from implementation activities.

---

## Governance Process

Every significant architectural modification follows the same process.

Proposal

↓

Architecture Review

↓

Impact Assessment

↓

Governance Approval

↓

Implementation

↓

Validation

↓

Certification

↓

Documentation Update

No architectural modification is considered complete until documentation reflects the approved design.

---

## Governance Scope

Architecture Governance reviews include:

- creation of new domains;
- domain ownership changes;
- dependency modifications;
- architectural restructuring;
- public contract evolution;
- governance policy updates.

Routine implementation improvements remain outside governance scope.

---

## Permanent Principles

Architecture Governance permanently enforces:

- Architecture First
- Explicit Ownership
- Stable Domain Boundaries
- Controlled Evolution
- Documentation First
- Governance Before Change

These principles remain valid throughout the lifetime of Scientific Graph AI.

---

## Review Criteria

Every architectural proposal shall be evaluated according to:

- architectural consistency;
- dependency integrity;
- domain ownership;
- maintainability;
- scalability;
- governance compliance.

Approval requires satisfactory evaluation of every applicable criterion.

---

## Success Criteria

Architecture Governance is considered successful when:

- architecture remains coherent;
- ownership remains explicit;
- dependencies remain stable;
- documentation reflects implementation;
- governance remains predictable;
- long-term maintainability improves.

---

## Long-Term Evolution

Future governance capabilities may incorporate:

- automated architectural validation;
- dependency analysis tools;
- governance dashboards;
- AI-assisted architectural review.

Regardless of future tooling, governance remains founded upon the principles established within MASTER_ROADMAP_V2.

---

## Conclusion

Architecture Governance preserves the architectural identity of Scientific Graph AI.

Every future architectural evolution shall strengthen the platform while preserving its long-term vision, structural consistency and engineering discipline.
